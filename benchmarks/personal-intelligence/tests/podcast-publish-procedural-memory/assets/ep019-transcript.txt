TITLE: Tools You Regret Building
DATE: 2026-08-04
DURATION: 39:47
SUMMARY:
Maya and Rob swap stories about the tools they wish they'd never built: the
dashboard nobody reads, the bot that pings at 3 a.m., the config system with
one user. A tool you have to babysit isn't a tool, it's a pet.

Rob proposes the one-month funeral rule for internal tools, and Maya argues
the real smell is any tool whose maintenance chat is busier than its users.
