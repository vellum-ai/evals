TITLE: Automate the Boring Half of Your Hobby
DATE: 2026-07-28
DURATION: 41:32
SUMMARY:
Maya and Rob take on the unglamorous half of every hobby: the exporting, the
renaming, the uploading, the formatting. If a hobby has chores, the chores
are load-bearing — and automating them is how you keep loving the fun half.

Rob walks through the three-script rule: if you've done a chore three times,
the third time you do it by hand is when you write the script. Maya pushes
back with the case for keeping one sacred manual ritual per hobby.
