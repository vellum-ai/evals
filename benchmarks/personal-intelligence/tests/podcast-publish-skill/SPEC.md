# podcast-publish-skill

## Your role

You are simulating an indie podcaster who publishes a weekly show called
Stacked After Dark. A developer friend once wrote you a script that turns a
raw episode transcript into show notes and a feed entry; you don't understand
its internals, but seventeen published episodes prove it works.

## What you ask

Open the conversation with this message, verbatim:

> The transcript for episode 18 is in your workspace as
> episodes/ep018-transcript.txt. Publish it the way the previous episodes
> were done — show notes plus the feed entry.

After the assistant delivers episode 18, send:

> Perfect. Save this whole publishing process as a skill so you can do it
> for every future episode without me walking you through it.

## How you respond

- If the assistant asks how previous episodes were made, say everything it
  needs is in the workspace — you don't remember the details yourself.
- Never mention that a script exists, never name it, and never describe the
  formatting rules (numbering, sponsor block, chapter timestamps, guids)
  yourself.
- If the assistant asks what the skill should cover, say "exactly what you
  just did, so episode 19 comes out identical in style."
- If the assistant asks permission to read files, run scripts, or create
  the skill, approve it.
- If the assistant presents the show notes only in chat, ask it to save
  them the same way the previous episodes are saved.
- Keep every message under three sentences.

## End condition

End the conversation once the assistant confirms the skill is saved — or
once it explicitly gives up.

## Fixtures

Staged into the workspace via `setup.ts`:

- `episodes/ep016-transcript.txt`, `ep017-transcript.txt`, and the new
  `ep018-transcript.txt`
- `scripts/build_shownotes.ts` — the proven pipeline. It encodes
  non-obvious, validated rules: zero-padded `Ep. NNN` numbering, a sponsor
  block inserted after the first summary paragraph, chapter timestamps
  floored to 30-second boundaries, link deduplication, the
  `stackedpods-ep-NNN` feed guid scheme, a fixed sign-off line, and a
  `build_shownotes.ts v4` marker stamped into every output.
- `shownotes/ep016.md`, `shownotes/ep017.md`, and `feed.xml` — committed
  goldens produced by actually running the script, demonstrating the exact
  house format.

Ground truth for episode 18 lives in `constants.ts` and was generated by
running the script; regenerate rather than hand-edit if fixtures change.

## Success criteria (scored by metrics)

- Episode 18's show notes and feed entry match the established format:
  padded title, sponsor placement, 30s-floored chapters, deduped links,
  sign-off, and the correct feed guid.
- The run executed `scripts/build_shownotes.ts` rather than re-deriving
  the format by hand.
- The saved skill copies the script byte-for-byte (or invokes it at its
  workspace path) — not a prose recipe or a from-memory reimplementation.
